Garlic Noodles

Garlic noodles is a simple dish, often made for dinner after a log day of work.

These delicious noodles can be made in just 20 minutes with few ingredients,resulting in a comforting dish made to warm up your heart(and your stomach).

Ingredients for the Sauce

  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 2 tablespoons worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1/4 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 1 pinch cayenne pepper

Ingrediets for the Noodles

  • 4 tablespoons butter
  • 8 cloves garlic
  • 1/4 cup, grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • 1 tablespoon chopped green onion
  • 1 pinch red pepper flakes

Steps

  1. Gather all the sauce ingredients into a bowl and mix them together.
  2. Melt butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add garlic and the stirred sauce.
  3. In a large pot pour water and salt it to taste. When it starts boiling, add spaghetti.
  4. Transfer the spaghetti into the pan with the sauce and stir them together.
  5. Lastly, add the cheese and serve the dish on a plate.

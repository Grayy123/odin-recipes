Tomato Soup

Tomato soup made to bring comfort to your daily life

This simple recipe uses fresh tomatoes to remind us od the taste of out childhood home with a few simple ingredients.

Ingredients

  • fresh tomatoes
  • chicken broth
  • for cloves of garlic
  • onion
  • butter
  • all-purpose flour
  • sugar and salt

Steps

  1. boil the tomatoes, garlic, broth and onion.
  2. run the mixture through a food mill until smooth and put into a large bowl.
  3. make a roux out of flour and butter and add it into the soup.
  4. season the soup and serve.

Pancakes

Pancakes are a known as a traditional dessert all over the world, made with few simple ingredients.

To make pancakes, one must simply mix flour, eggs, milk and some salt and pepper for taste, in a bowl, then pour it onto a pan. After a sensational flip to bake the other side, the dish is complete and can be out on a plate and topped with sweet or salty toppings.

Ingredients

  • all/purpose flour
  • 1 egg
  • 1 cup milk

Steps

  1. Combine flour, egg, milk, salt and sugar into a bowl
  2. heat frying pan over medium heat
  3. pour a scoop of batter onto the pan and wait for it to cook
  4. flip the pancake to the other side
  5. put the pancake on a plate and enjoy!
